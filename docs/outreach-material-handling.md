# Material Handling Partner Outreach — Brink Commercial

**The play in one line:** I bring material handling reps a weekly list of who's pulling commercial permits in their territory. They tell me when a customer says "we're out of room." Both of us get to the operator before the competition.

---

## 1. Compliance first (read before you pitch)

**Don't offer them cash for referrals.** North Carolina license law treats paying referral or "finder's" fees to unlicensed people as prohibited conduct ([NCREC bulletin](https://bulletins.ncrec.gov/2023/11), [NC Realtors](https://www.ncrealtors.org/?p=50901)). Run SC by Premier's broker-in-charge too.

So the deal is a **two-way referral and information swap, with no money changing hands**:
- You give them permit intel and send them every tenant or buyer who needs racking, forklifts, dock equipment or conveyor.
- They introduce you when a customer is outgrowing their building or relocating.

Run the final wording past Premier's broker-in-charge before you send anything.

## 3. Before the first email: build the "proof list"

Walk in with something real. Pull 30–60 days of commercial permits (run `pull_permits.py` or use the Mecklenburg "Building Permits Issued Daily" tool). Cut it down to the **10–15 best industrial and flex permits** for that rep's territory. That one sheet is the whole pitch.

Don't send the full list in the first email. Use it to get the meeting.

## 7. The meeting (20 minutes)

1. **Show the sheet** (5 min). Their territory, 10–15 permits. Ask: "How many of these do you already know?"
2. **Ask about their pipeline** (5 min). "Who are you quoting right now that's tight on space or talking about a second building?" Don't push for names; let them share what they're comfortable sharing.
3. **Agree the swap** (5 min):
   - You: a weekly or biweekly permit list for their territory, plus every tenant you place who needs equipment.
   - Them: a heads-up when a customer is outgrowing or relocating.
4. **Set the rhythm** (5 min). Who gets the list, how often (email is fine), and a 30-day check-in.

**After:** log it in the Partners table (see `2-airtable-setup.md`) and set a Todoist follow-up for 30 days.

---

## 8. What a referral from them looks like

Tell them exactly what to listen for so they know when to call you:
- "We're out of room" / "we're stacking in the aisles"
- "We're adding a shift / a new contract / a new product line"
- "We're looking at a second building"
- "Our lease is up in [year]"
- "Corporate is consolidating into the Carolinas"
- Quoting a rack layout for a building the customer **doesn't occupy yet**. That means a move is underway.
